SAM KAY ANIMAL FEEDS

WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT MASTER PROMPT

Project brief, functional requirements, design direction and
implementation instructions

1.  Project instruction

Act as a senior full-stack software engineer, UI/UX designer, e-commerce
architect, database engineer, SEO specialist and mobile-first web
developer. Plan, design and build a professional, secure, responsive
website for Sam Kay Animal Feeds. The website must make it easy for
customers to discover feeds for rabbits, dogs and cats, choose package
sizes and quantities, review an estimated order total, and complete
their order enquiry through WhatsApp.

Build a real, functional website—not just a static visual mock-up. Use
the business information and product details supplied by the owner as
the source of truth. Where information is missing, create editable
placeholders or request confirmation. Never invent product formulations,
ingredients, nutritional values, certifications, prices, delivery
promises or business claims.

2.  Business profile

3.  Main website goals

Present Sam Kay Animal Feeds as a credible, professional animal-feed
manufacturer and supplier.

Help visitors browse products by animal category: Rabbit, Dog and Cat.

Allow customers to select available package sizes and enter the
quantities they need.

Allow customers to add products from different categories to one basket.

Keep individual product prices hidden on public catalogue and
product-detail views.

Show an estimated order total in the basket/order summary, calculated
from current admin-managed prices and discounts.

Send the complete order enquiry to the business through WhatsApp. Do not
process online payments.

Provide a secure admin dashboard for managing products, package sizes,
prices, discounts, stock and business information.

Make the website fast, accessible, search-engine friendly and easy to
use on mobile devices.

5.  Homepage requirements

Create a clean, welcoming hero section with the business name, a concise
value proposition and clear calls to action such as “Explore Feeds” and
“Order on WhatsApp”.

Use the owner’s supplied logo. Do not recreate or replace it unless
asked.

Show three prominent category cards for Rabbit Feeds, Dog Feeds and Cat
Feeds.

Explain that the business manufactures and supplies animal feeds, using
only claims confirmed by the owner.

Highlight affordability, quality, wholesale availability and pickup at
Tanke.

Include a simple “How to Order” section: browse feeds, choose size and
quantity, review basket, send order on WhatsApp.

Include contact and WhatsApp calls to action, plus a clear footer.

6.  Feed catalogue and product details

Organise the catalogue into three categories:

Rabbit Feeds

Dog Feeds

Cat Feeds

The actual product names, descriptions, photographs, ingredients and
nutritional specifications must come from the owner’s product catalogue.
If that catalogue is not available during development, use clearly
labelled sample/demo entries only and do not publish them as real
products.

Each product record should support:

Product name and unique URL slug

Animal category

Short and full product descriptions

Product images and descriptive alt text

Available package sizes configured separately for each product

Admin-managed price for each package size

Stock status and optional stock quantity

Wholesale/bulk discount eligibility

Ingredients and feeding guidance only when verified by the owner

Nutritional information only when exact verified specifications are
supplied

Published/unpublished status

Important: Do not assume every product is available in every package
size. The admin must be able to configure package sizes independently
for each product.

7.  Package sizes and quantity selection

Initial package-size options:

1kg

2kg

5kg

10kg

20kg

25kg

Customers may order any quantity; there is no general minimum order
quantity. For each product, display only the package sizes enabled by
the administrator. Provide clear quantity controls, including plus/minus
buttons and a direct quantity field. Validate that quantities are whole
numbers greater than zero.

8.  Pricing and wholesale discounts

Do not show individual product or package prices on public catalogue
cards or product-detail pages.

Show the calculated estimated subtotal and order total in the
basket/order summary.

Prices must be entered and maintained by the administrator; do not
invent or hard-code real prices.

Allow the administrator to configure prices by product and package size.

Allow configurable bulk/wholesale discounts by product and quantity
threshold. Support percentage or fixed-amount discounts if appropriate.

Show a clear note that the basket total is an estimate and final
availability, delivery charges and order confirmation are subject to
business confirmation.

If a product/package has no configured price, do not calculate a false
total. Display “Request a quotation on WhatsApp” for that line item.

Calculate prices and discounts securely on the server using current
database values. Do not trust totals submitted by the browser.

9.  Basket and order summary (QUANTITY)

The basket must support:

Adding multiple products, including products from different animal
categories.

Selecting package size and quantity for each line item.

Increasing/decreasing quantities and removing items.

Showing product name, package size, quantity, line total, discounts,
subtotal and estimated total.

Persisting the basket during normal browsing, preferably with a suitable
client-side persistence approach.

Clear empty-basket and successful-add-to-basket feedback.

A customer notes/instructions field.

A fulfilment choice: pickup at Tanke or delivery (delivery details to be
confirmed/configured).

Customer name and phone number before preparing the WhatsApp enquiry.

10. WhatsApp order completion

WhatsApp is the final order-enquiry channel. There must be no online
checkout payment gateway at this stage. When the customer clicks the
WhatsApp order button, open WhatsApp Click to Chat with a pre-filled,
readable message.

The generated message should include:

Business name: Sam Kay Animal Feeds

Customer name and phone number

Each selected product name

Animal category

Package size and quantity

Line totals and any applicable discount, where prices are configured

Estimated order total, where calculable

Pickup or delivery preference

Delivery location, if applicable

Customer notes or special instructions

Use the confirmed WhatsApp number in international format without spaces
or punctuation in the wa.me URL. The currently supplied number must be
verified before launch. Opening WhatsApp does not mean an order has been
submitted or accepted; the customer must send the message, and the
business must confirm the order.

View basic order-enquiry activity or analytics if enquiry tracking is

12. Recommended technology

13. Suggested database structure

Design a relational schema with appropriate IDs, foreign keys,
timestamps and indexes. Suggested tables:

profiles — administrator profile and role information

categories — Rabbit, Dog and Cat categories

products — product details and publication status

product_images — product image paths, ordering and alt text

package_sizes — sizes enabled for each product

product_prices — private prices for product/package combinations

discount_rules — wholesale discount thresholds and values

inventory — stock quantities and availability

nutritional_info — verified nutritional specifications

faqs — editable frequently asked questions

business_settings — business contact, WhatsApp, pickup and delivery
settings

order_enquiries — optional enquiry records if the business wants to
track initiated enquiries

14. Security and privacy requirements

Enable Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) on relevant tables.

Public visitors may read published product/category information only.

Public users must not be able to read private product_prices, discount
rules or other confidential admin data directly.

Only authenticated, authorised administrators may create or change
products, prices, discounts, inventory and settings.

Never expose the Supabase service-role key or other secrets in
browser-side code.

Validate and calculate all prices and discounts server-side using
trusted database records.

Validate all user inputs and protect admin routes and server endpoints.

Do not collect unnecessary personal information. Provide suitable
privacy information.

15. Design and user experience

Use a modern, clean, trustworthy visual identity suitable for an
animal-feed manufacturer.

Use the supplied business logo and brand assets.

Use a consistent colour palette, readable typography, clear spacing and
high-quality product imagery supplied or approved by the owner.

Make category browsing and adding products to the basket simple and
obvious.

Use clear labels and feedback for size selection, quantity controls,
stock status and basket actions.

Keep the interface uncluttered; avoid excessive animations, pop-ups or
decorative elements that slow down ordering.

Make buttons and form controls touch-friendly and accessible.

16. Mobile responsiveness and performance

Design mobile-first and support screen widths from approximately 320px
upward.

Use responsive layouts for phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Prevent horizontal scrolling and clipped content.

Make navigation, product cards, quantity controls and WhatsApp actions
easy to use on small screens.

Optimise images, lazy-load below-the-fold media and minimise unnecessary
scripts.

Aim for strong Core Web Vitals: LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, INP below
200 milliseconds and CLS below 0.1 under suitable test conditions.

17. SEO requirements

Use descriptive page titles and meta descriptions for the business and
its product categories.

Use semantic HTML headings and meaningful internal links.

Create readable product and category URLs.

Add descriptive alt text to product images.

Generate an XML sitemap and configure robots.txt.

Add canonical URLs and Open Graph metadata where appropriate.

Use structured data only when the information is accurate and eligible;
do not publish fabricated ratings, reviews or prices.

Prepare the site for Google Search Console and analytics integration.

18. Accessibility

Use accessible colour contrast and readable text sizes.

Ensure all interactive controls can be used with a keyboard.

Provide visible focus states and descriptive form labels.

Use meaningful link and button text.

Provide useful error messages and status feedback.

Do not rely on colour alone to communicate stock or validation status.

21. Important rules — do not violate

Do not display individual product prices on public product listings or
product pages.

Do show an estimated total in the basket when valid prices are
configured.

Do not invent product names, feed formulations, ingredients, nutritional
values, certifications, prices or reviews.

Do not assume all products have all package sizes.

Do not impose a general minimum order quantity.

Do not add an online payment gateway; orders are finalised through
WhatsApp.

Do not expose private pricing or administrator credentials to public
visitors.

Do not treat opening WhatsApp as a confirmed order.

Keep the main navigation limited to Home, Feeds and Quantity.
